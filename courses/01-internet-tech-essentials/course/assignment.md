# Assignment: Prove You Understand the Basics

**Internet & Tech Essentials · Coraxshift DevOps Academy** · ⏱️ 2 to 3 hours · Level: beginner

## 🎯 What this is

You will prove you understand the foundations by **explaining them, drawing them, and showing evidence from your own computer**. Nobody is looking for perfect English or perfect drawings. They are looking for your own understanding.

## 📖 The scenario

A friend runs **CoraxReads**, a small online bookstore hosted in Lagos. A customer in the USA wants to open it. Your job is to explain how that works, compare two ways of building the app, choose the right DNS record, set up your tools, and share what you learned.

## ✅ Rules

1. **Write in your own words.** Do not copy an AI answer.
2. **Every diagram must be yours.** Hand-drawn or on [draw.io](https://app.diagrams.net/), both are fine.
3. **Use your own computer for screenshots.** Blur your username and personal details if you want.
4. **No passwords, tokens or keys** in any screenshot.
5. Keep Tasks 1 to 6 **in order**, in one folder, with clear headings.

## 📋 What you need

- Your portfolio repository (see [Getting Started](../GETTING-STARTED.md))
- A browser, and VS Code (desktop or Codespace)
- An AI assistant for Task 1. LinkedIn is optional. Task 6 has a private option.

## Task 1: Use an AI assistant

**Goal:** write a clear prompt and learn one idea well.

1. Open an AI assistant.
2. Write a prompt like this in your own words: *"Explain TCP/IP to a beginner. Use simple language and one real-life example, like sending a letter or making a phone call."*
3. Read the answer. Does it have a simple definition and a real-life example?
4. Save your prompt, and write a short note about one thing you learned.

**Submit:** your prompt and your learning note. A screenshot is optional (never include private details).

## Task 2: Internet and networking

**Goal:** explain in **100 to 150 words** how a user in the USA can open a website hosted in Lagos.

Your explanation must cover, in your own words:

- Packet switching
- IP address
- TCP/IP
- HTTP and HTTPS

**Submit:** your explanation under a **Task 2** heading.

## Task 3: App architecture

**Goal:** compare a two-tier and a three-tier version of CoraxReads with **your own diagrams**.

1. Draw a **two-tier** diagram: Frontend and Database. Label every part. Show the arrows.
2. Draw a **three-tier** diagram: Frontend, Backend and Database. Label every part. Show the arrows.
3. Beside **Frontend**, list at least 2 technologies. (For example: HTML, React)
4. Beside **Backend**, list at least 2 technologies. (For example: Node.js, Python)
5. Beside **Database**, list at least 2 technologies. (For example: PostgreSQL, MongoDB)

**Submit:** a screenshot or photo of both diagrams, with labels readable.

## Task 4: Domain name and DNS

**Scenario:** CoraxReads can be opened today by the IP address `102.130.45.78` on port `3000`. Your friend bought the domain `coraxreads.com`.

**Goal:** explain DNS and choose the right record.

1. In **50 to 100 words**, explain what DNS is, in your own words.
2. Say which DNS record your friend should add to connect `coraxreads.com` to `102.130.45.78`, and why.
3. Keep it to explaining. You do not need to change any real DNS settings.

**Submit:** your explanation and your record answer.

> 💡 DNS connects the domain to the IP address. The port (3000) is handled separately, by the app or web server, not by DNS.

## Task 5: VS Code setup, hands-on

**Goal:** set up VS Code and prove it is yours.

1. Open VS Code (desktop or Codespace). Pick a theme you like.
2. Open the built-in terminal: **Terminal → New Terminal**.
3. Run the commands for your system:
   - Windows: `whoami`, then `dir`
   - Mac, Linux or Codespace: `whoami`, then `pwd`, then `ls`
4. Take **one screenshot** that shows the editor, your theme and the terminal output.

**Submit:** your screenshot. Blur your username or personal details if you do not want them public.

## Task 6: Share what you learned

**Goal:** summarise Tasks 1 to 5 in your own words. Publishing is **optional**.

1. Write a short post or reflection with these headings: *Learning assistant, Internet and networking, Architecture, DNS, VS Code setup*.
2. Write it in your own words. Add one or two screenshots only if they show nothing personal.
3. **Option A (public):** post it on LinkedIn, add the credit line below, and save the link.
4. **Option B (private):** save the reflection in your portfolio folder. Do not publish anything you do not want public.

Credit line for a public post:

```text
P.S. This post is part of Internet & Tech Essentials at Coraxshift DevOps Academy.
Learn by building: https://coraxshift-academy.github.io/
```

**Submit:** the LinkedIn link, or your private reflection.

## 📦 Submission checklist

- [ ] Tasks 1 to 6 in order, in one folder: `essentials/assignment/`
- [ ] Task 1: prompt and learning note
- [ ] Task 2: 100 to 150 words that mention packet switching, IP, TCP/IP and HTTP/HTTPS
- [ ] Task 3: labelled two-tier and three-tier diagrams, with technology examples
- [ ] Task 4: 50 to 100 words and the correct record (A record)
- [ ] Task 5: screenshot with editor, theme and terminal output
- [ ] Task 6: reflection (LinkedIn link only if you chose to post publicly)
- [ ] No passwords, keys, tokens or private information
- [ ] Everything is in your own words

## 📤 Final submission

Send your instructor the link to your `essentials/assignment/` folder in your portfolio.
