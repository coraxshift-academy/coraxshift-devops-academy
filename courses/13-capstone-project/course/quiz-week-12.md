# Quiz Week 12: Capstone Operations

12 questions, 20 minutes. 8 Choose ONE, 4 Choose ALL that apply. Pass: 11 out of 12.

## Questions

Q1 - Choose ONE
What should an architecture diagram show?
A. The actual components and their request paths
B. Only a decorative logo
C. Credentials
D. Every learner's personal data
Answer: A. Explanation: A useful diagram describes real components and how traffic moves.

Q2 - Choose ONE
What proves a README is usable?
A. A peer can follow it from a clean checkout
B. It has many pages
C. It says "works on my machine"
D. It omits prerequisites
Answer: A. Explanation: A clean reproduction tests whether instructions are complete.

Q3 - Choose ONE
What is the first goal during a service incident?
A. Protect users and assess impact
B. Find someone to blame
C. Delete logs
D. Hide the alert
Answer: A. Explanation: Incident response prioritizes safety and user impact.

Q4 - Choose ONE
Why verify health after rollback?
A. Confirm recovery actually worked
B. Make the dashboard colorful
C. Avoid checking logs
D. Change Git history
Answer: A. Explanation: A rollback command is not proof that the service recovered.

Q5 - Choose ONE
What belongs in an incident update?
A. Impact, evidence, action, and current status
B. Passwords and tokens
C. Blame
D. Unverified guesses as facts
Answer: A. Explanation: Factual updates help stakeholders understand status without exposing secrets.

Q6 - Choose ONE
Why preserve immutable image identifiers?
A. Know exactly what was tested and deployed
B. Make every image public
C. Replace source control
D. Hide the build time
Answer: A. Explanation: Immutable identifiers provide artifact traceability.

Q7 - Choose ONE
What is a budget alert?
A. A notification, not a guaranteed spending cap
B. An automatic deletion policy
C. A free-tier promise
D. An SSH key
Answer: A. Explanation: Alerts can arrive late and do not necessarily stop resource usage.

Q8 - Choose ONE
What is a good portfolio demo?
A. Show evidence, explain tradeoffs, and state limitations
B. Claim zero risk
C. Hide all failures
D. Show credentials to prove access
Answer: A. Explanation: Honest evidence and limitations show engineering judgment.

Q9 - Choose ALL that apply
What belongs in a capstone README?
A. Prerequisites and startup steps
B. Tests and expected results
C. Monitoring and recovery notes
D. Real passwords
Answers: A, B, C. Explanation: Reproducible instructions, validation, and operations help users; secrets must not be published.

Q10 - Choose ALL that apply
What should be checked during cloud cleanup?
A. Compute instances
B. Storage and snapshots
C. Public IP allocations/load balancers
D. Only the local browser
Answers: A, B, C. Explanation: Many related resources can incur charges after compute stops.

Q11 - Choose ALL that apply
Which are good incident practices?
A. Capture a timeline
B. Preserve useful sanitized evidence
C. Verify service after recovery
D. Delete logs before review
Answers: A, B, C. Explanation: Timeline, evidence, and verification support recovery and learning.

Q12 - Choose ALL that apply
What makes a runbook useful?
A. Clear first checks
B. Exact safe recovery steps
C. Owner and escalation path
D. Unexplained destructive commands
Answers: A, B, C. Explanation: Runbooks should be actionable, owned, and safe; unexplained deletion is dangerous.

## Scoring

11 to 12: Pass, earn Week 12 badge. Below 11: review the explanations and retake once.
