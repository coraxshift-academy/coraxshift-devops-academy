# Role Play: Explain DNS to a Shop Owner

**Internet & Tech Essentials · Coraxshift DevOps Academy** · ⏱️ 15 minutes (after Class 3)

## 🎭 The scenario

A shop owner runs a small clothing shop in Lagos. She wants a website, so customers can find her online. She asks you:

> *"What is DNS? And why do I need a domain?"*

She is clever, but she is not technical. She does not know these words, and she will stop you if you use one without explaining it.

## 🎯 Your job

Explain DNS and domains in **about one minute**, in plain words, with **no unexplained jargon**.

## 💡 A way to think about it

- A **domain** is the name of your shop on the internet, so people can find you easily.
- **DNS** is the contacts list. People remember names. Computers need number addresses. DNS connects the two.

> 📖 **From my café:** This is how I would explain it to a customer: "Every website lives at a number, but nobody remembers numbers. DNS is the phone book. You type the name, and it finds the number for you."

## 🧪 A starting script (say it in your own words)

> "When you want to call a friend, you don't remember their phone number. You look up their name in your phone, and your phone finds the number. DNS does the same thing for websites. A customer types the name of your shop, like `ladyshop.com`, and DNS finds the number address of the computer where your website lives. A domain is simply the name you choose for your shop, so people can find you without remembering a number."

Do **not** read it out. Say it in your own words. If you read it out, it will not help you.

## ✅ Pass check

- [ ] You spoke for about one minute
- [ ] You did not use "IP", "DNS" or "server" without explaining it
- [ ] The listener could repeat your explanation in her own words
- [ ] You used one everyday comparison
