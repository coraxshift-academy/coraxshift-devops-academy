

# **Assignment 1: Basic Understanding of Internet, Networking and Tools Basics**

Time: 120 to 180 minutes
Level: Beginner
Submit in: Your portfolio repo, in folder modules/01-essentials/assignment-1

## **Overview**

You are starting your DevOps journey. You will prove you understand the foundations by explaining them, drawing them, and showing evidence from your own computer.

## **Scenario**

You are supporting CoraxReads, a small online bookstore built by CoraxShift. It is hosted in Lagos. A user in the USA wants to open it. Your job is to explain how that works, compare architectures, fix the DNS, set up your tools, and share what you learned.

## **Learning Outcomes**

By the end, you will be able to:
1. Use an AI learning assistant with a clear prompt
2. Explain how internet communication works across countries
3. Explain packet switching, IP address, TCP/IP, and HTTP/HTTPS in your own words
4. Compare two-tier and three-tier architecture with original diagrams
5. Explain DNS and pick the right record for an IPv4 address
6. Set up VS Code and verify it with your own terminal
7. Share your learning in a clear public post or private portfolio reflection

## **Rules**

1. Write in your own words. Do not copy ChatGPT output as your answer.
2. All diagrams must be yours. Hand drawn or draw.io, both are fine.
3. Use your own computer for screenshots. You may blur your username and other personal details before sharing them.
4. Do not paste passwords, tokens, or keys in any screenshot.
5. Keep Tasks 1 to 6 in order, in one folder, with clear headings.

## **What you need**

Read the [Getting Started environment and software guide](../../GETTING-STARTED.md) first. You need internet, a browser, and access to the course repository. VS Code desktop is optional if you use the Codespaces editor. The AI activity can use ChatGPT or another instructor-approved assistant. ## **Task 1 - Use an AI Learning Assistant**

Goal: Write a clear prompt and learn one concept well.
Steps:
1. Open ChatGPT or another instructor-approved learning assistant. If you do not have access, ask your instructor to demonstrate the prompt and response.
2. Write a prompt like this, in your own words: Explain TCP/IP to a beginner. Use beginner-friendly language and one real-life example, like sending a letter or making a phone call.
3. Read the answer. Check that it has a simple definition and a real-life example.
4. Save the prompt and write a short note explaining one thing you learned. A screenshot is optional; never include private account or personal information.
What to submit: Your prompt and learning note; optional screenshot.

## **Task 2 - Internet and Networking**

Scenario: Your friend runs CoraxReads in Lagos. A user in the USA wants to open it.
Goal: Explain in 100 to 150 words how that user in the USA can open a website hosted in Lagos.
Your explanation must clearly cover, in your own words: Packet switching, IP address, TCP/IP, HTTP/HTTPS
What to submit: Your 100 to 150 word explanation, under Task 2 heading. No screenshot needed.

## **Task 3 - Application Architecture and Stack**

Scenario: CoraxReads has two versions.

Two-tier: Frontend and Database.

Three-tier: Frontend, Backend, and Database.
Goal: Compare them with original diagrams.
Steps:
1. Draw a two-tier diagram. Show Frontend and Database. Label every layer. Show arrows for communication.
2. Draw a three-tier diagram. Show Frontend, Backend, and Database. Label every layer. Show arrows.
3. For Frontend, list at least 2 technologies. Example: HTML, React.
4. For Backend, list at least 2 technologies. Example: Node.js, Python.
5. For Database, list at least 2 technologies. Example: PostgreSQL, MongoDB.
What to submit: Screenshot 2 - Your two diagrams, with labels visible.

## **Task 4 - Domain Name and DNS**

Scenario: CoraxReads can be opened today by IP 102.130.45.78 at port 3000. Your friend bought the domain coraxreads.com.
Goal: Explain DNS and pick the right record.
Steps:
1. In 50 to 100 words, in your own words, explain what DNS is.
2. Say which DNS record your friend should use to connect coraxreads.com to 102.130.45.78, and why.
3. Keep it conceptual. You do not need to configure DNS in this task.
What to submit: Your 50 to 100 word explanation plus your record answer.
Note: DNS maps the domain to the IP. Port 3000 is handled separately by the app or web server, not by DNS.

## **Task 5 - VS Code Setup, Hands-On**

Goal: Set up VS Code and prove it is yours.
Steps:
1. Use VS Code desktop or the Codespaces editor. Install VS Code locally only if you chose the optional local path in the setup guide.
2. Open the editor. Pick a theme you like.
3. Open the integrated terminal: Terminal menu, then New Terminal.
4. Run a command for your system:
   - Windows PowerShell: `whoami`, then `dir`
   - Mac or Linux: `whoami`, then `pwd`, then `ls`
   - Codespace: `whoami`, then `pwd`, then `ls`

5. Take a screenshot showing the editor, your theme, and terminal output. Blur your username or other personal details if you do not want them public.
What to submit: Screenshot 3 - Your editor window with theme and terminal output. Blur personal details if you do not want them public.
