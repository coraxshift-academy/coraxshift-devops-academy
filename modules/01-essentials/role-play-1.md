

# **Role Play 1: Explaining Technical Concepts to a Non-Technical Stakeholder**

Time: 15 minutes in Class 3

## **Scenario**

A shop owner runs a small shop in Lagos. She wants a website for her shop. She asks: What is DNS? Why do I need a domain?

## **Your job**

Explain DNS in 1 minute, in plain words, no jargon. Use the phonebook example. Do not say IP address without explaining it.

## **Example script in your own words**

You can use this as a guide, then say it in your own words:
When you want to call a friend, you do not remember their phone number. You search their name in your phone contacts, and your phone finds the number. DNS is the same. You type a name like coraxreads.com. DNS finds the number address for that name, so your browser can find the shop website. A domain is the name you pick for your shop, so people can find you easily.

## **Pass check**

- You spoke for about 1 minute
- You used no jargon without explaining it
- The shop owner can repeat it back in her own words
- You did not say IP, DNS, server, without explaining it

## **What to push**

Write your 1 minute script in your portfolio, in your own words.
