# Role Play 2: Setting up Nginx Web Server for Mini Finance
Time: 15 minutes in Class 3

## Scenario
A small finance shop wants a website. The owner asks: What is Nginx? Why do we need it? Can you set it up?

## Your job
Explain Nginx in 1 minute, in plain words, no jargon. Use a waiter example: Nginx is like a waiter who takes orders and brings food from the kitchen to the customer.

## Example script in your own words
When you open a website, your browser asks for a page. Nginx is the waiter. It takes your request, goes to the kitchen, which is the folder /var/www/html, gets the page, and brings it to you. If the waiter is not working, you get no page. We start the waiter with service nginx start, and we test it with nginx -t.

## Pass check
- You spoke for about 1 minute
- You used no jargon without explaining it
- The owner can repeat it back
- You mentioned web root in plain words
