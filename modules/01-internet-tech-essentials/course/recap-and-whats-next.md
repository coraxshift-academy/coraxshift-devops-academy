# Recap and What's Next

**Internet & Tech Essentials · Coraxshift DevOps Academy · Learn. Build. Deploy. Grow.**

## 🏅 You did it

If you finished the assignment, the role play and the quiz, you went from "I use the internet" to "I know how it works". That is a real foundation.

Scored 11 out of 12 or more in the [quiz](quiz.md)? Congratulations: you have earned the **Internet & Tech Essentials** badge.

## What you can now do

- Explain what happens when you type `google.com`, using packets, IP addresses, DNS and HTTPS
- Use `ping` and `nslookup`, and say what the answers mean
- Choose the right DNS record, and explain why an **A record** is used for an IPv4 address
- Explain the difference between two-tier and three-tier apps
- Use VS Code and its terminal
- Ask an AI assistant good questions, and check what it says
- Troubleshoot in five steps, and ask for help clearly

> 📖 **From my café:** This course is for anyone starting from zero, including people who have never thought of themselves as technical. I learned much of this by watching, trying and fixing, one machine at a time, not in a classroom. You do not have to be a genius. You have to be curious and willing to look under the hood. I do not want you to simply use technology. I want you to understand it.
